# retouch: change history

What changed in each version, newest first. Each entry gives the version, the date and time it was released (Singapore time, 24-hour) and what changed. Why each change was made, and what Gary's runs on the Pixel showed, is in CLAUDE.md.

## v0.11.0, 30/10/26 1827 hrs, V4
- Video: skin smoothing on every frame, and the filter runs on the graphics chip.

## v0.10.0, 30/10/26 1717 hrs, V3
- Video: Size (long edge and frame rate) and Canvas tools.

## v0.9.3, 30/10/26 1614 hrs
- The page height comes from the window, not 100dvh, so the layout is right after an update reload.

## v0.9.2, 30/10/26 1607 hrs
- Version bump only, to trigger an update on the Pixel.

## v0.9.1, 30/10/26 1225 hrs
- Video: offers an exact cut when a split or trim start would snap far from where it was asked.

## v0.9.0, 30/10/26 1105 hrs, V2
- Video: cut and join clips, with an H.264 re-encode when the clips differ.

## v0.8.0, 30/10/26 0111 hrs, V1
- Video: video check, open, trim, sound and lossless export.
- The video check from the empty screen shows no edit count in the strip.
- Undo and redo of video edits have their own wording.

## v0.7.0, 26/10/26 2352 hrs
- Cut out: removes any background, picking the subject by itself.

## v0.6.2, 26/10/26 2022 hrs

## v0.6.1, 24/10/26 1616 hrs

## v0.6.0, 24/10/26 1431 hrs

## v0.5.2, 24/10/26 1120 hrs
